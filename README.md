## 📌 About Me

I'm a Data Engineer with 3+ years of experience building pipelines, data models, and dashboards that Operations, Finance, and leadership teams rely on every day. I hold an M.S. in **Information Technology & Analytics (GPA 3.9)** from RIT. Most recently at **Pacific Gas & Electric**, I built inspection data pipelines and self-service reporting in **Palantir Foundry**.

A few results I'm proud of:
- Delivered **$3M+** in cost savings with a Foundry pipeline processing **50M+ inspection records a year**
- Cut reporting turnaround by **75%** across **5 business verticals** at PG&E
- Led a **zero-downtime** SAP-to-AWS migration for a **120+ truck** fleet at **99.2%** data consistency
- Saved **$400K a year** with anomaly detection on fleet sensor data

I work across **Python, SQL, and PySpark**; **Palantir Foundry, AWS, Snowflake, and Databricks**; **Airflow and dbt**; **Power BI, Tableau, and Grafana**; and GenAI tooling like **AWS Bedrock and LangGraph**. I own pipelines end to end, from raw ingestion to the dashboards people act on.
